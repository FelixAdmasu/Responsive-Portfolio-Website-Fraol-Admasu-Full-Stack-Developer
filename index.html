Work Experience

Data Entry Specialist (Volunteer) — Mesob System Builder

Shager City First Service Center MESOB, Addis Ababa Dec 2025 - Present

In the Mezgeb (መዝገብ) — the records department of Sheger City's First Service Center — data entry staff face an endless cycle. Every day, truckloads of paper permits arrive from the Construction Department. Each one must be scanned, typed into Excel by hand, renamed by permit code, and filed into the correct sub-city folder. It is repetitive. It is error-prone. And it is painfully slow.

I asked a simple question: What if the data was digital from the moment it arrived? That question became Mesob — a permit management platform purpose-built for Ethiopian city government workflows. Built with Laravel 12, MySQL, Tailwind CSS, and Alpine.js. Handles 21,000+ construction permits with interactive geospatial mapping, 3-language interface, PDF certificate generation, and role-based access control.
